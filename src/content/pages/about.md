## My Connection to Brazil

I was born and raised in Brazil, and I lived there for 27 years. I served my mission in Brazil, I got married there, and both of my children, Vitor and Rebeca, were born there. My parents, grandparents, sister, nephews, and brother-in-law live there.

## An Interesting Fact About Brazil's Physical Geography

<!-- DRAFT: verify the numbers with the source on the References page before publishing. -->

The Amazon River, which flows across northern Brazil, carries more water than any other river on Earth. At Óbidos, about 970 kilometers upstream from its mouth, its flow has been measured at about 216,000 cubic meters of water per second, and the river carries about one fifth of all the water that runs off Earth's surface. So much fresh water pours into the Atlantic Ocean that it dilutes the ocean's saltiness for more than 160 kilometers from shore ([Crist et al., n.d.](/ps120/references/#ref-crist)).

## How the Earth Testifies of a Creator (Alma 30:44)

<!-- DRAFT from your plan. Adjust it so it reflects your own conviction. -->

Alma taught Korihor that "all things denote there is a God; yea, even the earth, and all things that are upon the face of it, yea, and its motion, yea, and also all the planets which move in their regular form do witness that there is a Supreme Creator" (Alma 30:44). When I study how the Earth works, I see order and purpose. The tilt of the Earth and its orbit around the Sun give us the seasons, the water cycle moves fresh water from the oceans to the land, and gravity keeps the planets on predictable paths. In Brazil, the same rains that feed the Amazon also sustain the forests, farms, and cities of an entire continent. These systems are complex, yet they work together so that life can exist. To me, this order is a witness of a Creator who designed the Earth for His children.
